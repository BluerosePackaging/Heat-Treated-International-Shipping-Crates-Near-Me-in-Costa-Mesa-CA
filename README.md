## Understanding ISPM 15 Requirements for International Shipping

ISPM 15 is an international phytosanitary standard that regulates solid wood packaging materials used in international trade. It aims to reduce the spread of pests through wooden packaging and related shipping materials.

For wood packaging covered by the standard, heat treatment must achieve a minimum wood-core temperature of 56°C for at least 30 minutes. Compliant packaging must also carry an authorized ISPM 15 mark applied through an approved process.

Important considerations include:

- Solid-wood packaging may include crates, pallets, dunnage, and wooden supports.
- Applicable wood packaging must meet the relevant treatment and marking requirements.
- The official mark identifies the country, authorized facility, and treatment method.
- The destination country's import requirements should be checked before shipment.
- Repairs or modifications to treated wood packaging may affect its compliance status.

Certain processed wood materials, including qualifying plywood and particleboard products, may be exempt from ISPM 15. Requirements depend on the material and destination country's regulations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What are heat treated international shipping crates?
These are wooden shipping crates made using wood packaging material that has undergone heat treatment. They are used for export shipments where treated solid-wood packaging is required.

### 2. What is ISPM 15?
ISPM 15 is an international standard regulating solid-wood packaging materials used in international trade. It establishes treatment and marking requirements intended to reduce the movement of wood pests between countries.

### 3. What temperature is required for ISPM 15 heat treatment?
The standard heat treatment schedule requires the wood core to reach at least 56°C for a minimum of 30 minutes.

### 4. Do all international shipments require heat treated wooden crates?
Not every shipment has the same requirements. The destination country's regulations, packaging material, and applicable exemptions determine whether ISPM 15 treatment is necessary.

### 5. How can I identify an ISPM 15-compliant crate?
Look for the authorized IPPC mark showing the country code, facility identification number, and treatment abbreviation, such as HT for heat treatment.

### 6. Can heat treated crates be customized?
Crates can be designed around cargo dimensions, weight, lifting requirements, and transportation conditions. Custom configurations may include enclosed walls, open-slat structures, and internal supports.

### 7. Are heat treated crates suitable for heavy machinery?
They can be designed for machinery and industrial equipment when the base, framing, and support structure are selected according to the cargo weight and handling requirements.

### 8. Can treated wooden crates be reused?
ISPM 15-marked wood packaging may generally be reused if it remains compliant, the mark is legible, and it has not been altered or repaired in a way that affects compliance. It should also be free from signs of pest infestation.

### 9. Do plywood crates require ISPM 15 treatment?
Qualifying processed wood materials, such as plywood manufactured using glue, heat, or pressure, are generally exempt from ISPM 15. However, solid-wood components used in the same packaging may still be subject to the standard.
